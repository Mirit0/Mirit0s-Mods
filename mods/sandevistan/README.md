# Sandevistan for Capri

Gives Capri a Sandevistan, activates when moving

- Kind: code
- Made by: Mirit0
- Version: 1.5.0

## What it does

Capri leaves a trail of afterimages behind her whenever she moves fast. Drag her across the screen, or throw her, and she activates her Sandy

Afterimages use RAM

Tested for windows only.
